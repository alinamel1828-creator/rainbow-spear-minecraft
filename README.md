# 🌈 Rainbow Spear (Minecraft Java 1.21.11)

Custom Datapack & Resource Pack adding the legendary **Rainbow Spear** with custom 16x16 pixel art, crafting recipe, and 6 unique PvP abilities.

## ✨ Features & Abilities
- **Passive (In Inventory):** Speed II, Jump Boost II, and rainbow dust trail.
- **Prism Shield (In Inventory):** Automatically grants Resistance III (10s) & Regeneration II when HP drops below 4 hearts.
- **Totem Escape (In Inventory):** Teleports the player 20–50 blocks away (Chorus Fruit style) upon popping a Totem of Undying.
- **Rainbow Dash (Mainhand + Shift):** Launches the player forward with a Warden Sonic Boom wave and Wind Burst.
- **Lightning Strike & Trap (On Hit):** Summons lightning, grants Strength II (10s), inflicts Slowness IV (5s), and traps the target in a temporary 2x2 Cobweb cage.
- **Rainbow Slam (Crit / Mid-air Hit):** Triggers an explosive mace-style shockwave.

## 📦 Installation
1. Download `RainbowSpear-Datapack-1.21.11.zip` and `RainbowSpear-ResourcePack-1.21.11.zip` from **Releases**.
2. Put the datapack into `.minecraft/saves/<YourWorld>/datapacks/` and run `/reload`.
3. Put the resource pack into `.minecraft/resourcepacks/` and enable it in settings.
